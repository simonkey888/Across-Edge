# Economics

Across-Edge does not invent profitability. Canonical observed fields are normalized from the pinned relayer when present:

- gross relayer fee USD;
- destination native/gas cost USD;
- canonical net relayer fee USD;
- output amount / capital required USD;
- repayment chain and LP fee data.

Rebalance/repayment realization cost remains `UNKNOWN` until independently measurable. Therefore a canonical net relayer fee is not automatically treated as final post-rebalance profit. Break-even scenarios at $0/$5/$10/$20 are derived calculations only and remain `UNKNOWN` when the required real net-per-fill evidence is missing.
